Who We Are

Temple of Learning & Comprehensive Development

Sri Little Angels High School, run by Dhanvika Educational Society, stands as a premier educational institution in Uppalapadu, Nellore, Andhra Pradesh. We are committed to fostering academic brilliance, creative discovery, and personal integrity in all our students.

Our approach integrates cutting-edge pedagogical tools with interactive classroom environments to make learning a joyful, stress-free experience. We prepare children not just for academic examinations, but for life challenges.

Character & Integrity

Instilling a high moral compass, social responsibility, and cultural values in children.

Conceptual & Practical Learning

Encouraging critical thinking, science projects, and experimental studies over rote learning.

Admission Procedure & Steps

Securing a place for your child at Sri Little Angels High School is a smooth, structured process. We offer registrations from Kindergarten up to Class 10. Start by submitting an inquiry, and our school administrative office will guide you.

01

Submit Registration Form

Fill the online form on this page or collect a physical form from the school office desk.

02

Interactive Dialogue

A friendly chat with the parents and children to understand prior learnings and interest areas.

03

Enrollment Finalization

Deposit required documents (Transfer Certificate, Birth Certificate, Aadhaar Card) and clear enrollment fee.

Registration Inquiry Sent!

Thank you for your interest. Our school representative will call you on the provided phone number shortly.
